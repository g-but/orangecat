# OrangeCat Go-to-Market — August 2026

**Created**: 2026-08-13
**Owner**: distribution engine (this doc ships alongside PR "feat: distribution engine")
**Scope**: one page. What we sell, to whom, through which channels, measured how.

---

## ICP

**Indie builders and creators who want to get paid without platforms.** Solo
developers, writers, artists, open-source maintainers, small service
providers — people who already have an audience or a client and lose margin,
sovereignty, or eligibility to the middleman between them. Sub-segment with the
sharpest pain: the pseudonymous and the "category-ineligible" (people payment
processors decline or freeze), for whom OrangeCat is not cheaper but *possible*.

## Positioning

**Your pay link, no middleman.** Everyone on OrangeCat owns
`orangecat.ch/pay/<username>` — a payment page that needs no account from the
payer, takes custody of nothing, and charges 0%. The broader platform (funding,
lending, investing, the Cat) is the roadmap; the pay link is the wedge, because
it is the smallest unit of the mission a stranger can verify in thirty seconds.

Against processors: they sell custody (approval, fees, freezes, settlement
delay); we sell a coordination surface. Against invoicing SaaS: no
subscription, no expiring invoices — alias-backed links that live in a bio.

## First-customer path (shortest loop)

1. A creator signs up → their `/pay/<username>` page exists immediately.
2. They share the link once (bio, chat, invoice footer).
3. Someone pays them a real amount over Lightning.

That third step is the activation event this GTM optimizes for. Everything
below exists to put more pay links in front of more payers.

## Channels, in leverage order

1. **Blog + RSS + SEO** *(fixed as of this PR)* — sitemap now carries all
   posts/articles, `/rss.xml` exists, content pages have canonical + OG
   metadata. Weekly essay cadence compounds here first; it is the only channel
   we fully own.
2. **Share-ready OG cards** *(shipped in this PR)* — every blog post, article,
   and profile now renders a real card in chats and feeds. Sharing is the
   distribution act our users already perform; the cards make each share carry
   the brand.
3. **Nostr as the native social channel** *(next step — not wired in this PR)*
   — `publishEvent()` exists at `src/lib/nostr/relays.ts:162` with **zero call
   sites**. Wiring publish-on-post would put every public article on the one
   social network whose audience is exactly our ICP, with zero platform risk.
   First concrete task: publish new public articles as long-form Nostr events.
4. **Cross-post strategy essays to X/LinkedIn** — manual, lowest leverage, but
   where the indie-builder audience currently is. Cross-post the weekly essay
   with the OG card and a link back; never write natively there.

## Weekly cadence

- **1 essay/week** on the blog (strategy or build-in-public; the voice of the
  existing posts), published Wednesday.
- **Same day**: cross-post to X/LinkedIn; once channel 3 ships, mirror to Nostr
  automatically.
- **Monthly**: one product-truth post (what shipped, real numbers — the
  changelog voice).
